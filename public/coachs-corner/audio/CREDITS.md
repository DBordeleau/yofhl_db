# Tie rustle recordings

All three source recordings are CC0 1.0. These edits use Freesound’s public high-quality MP3 previews, not original WAV downloads.

The excerpts are trimmed, filtered at 180–6000 Hz, faded, and matched to -26 dBFS RMS (with a -6 dBFS peak ceiling). Outputs are mono 48 kHz, 16-bit WAV. No synthetic mechanical layer is added.

- **tie-sleeve.wav**: [Sleeve rustle](https://freesound.org/people/martian/sounds/19291/) by martian. Excerpt 0.48–0.84 seconds. [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
- **tie-cloth-7.wav**: [Cloth rustle 7](https://freesound.org/people/zazz.sound.design/sounds/435292/) by zazz.sound.design. Excerpt 49.44–49.84 seconds. [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
- **tie-cloth-8.wav**: [Cloth rustle 8](https://freesound.org/people/zazz.sound.design/sounds/435296/) by zazz.sound.design. Excerpt 2.53–2.99 seconds. [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

Source hashes and exact processing settings are in `tie-rustles.json`. Rebuild with `node scripts/audio/prepare-tie-rustles.mjs`; see `scripts/audio/README.md` for source retrieval and FFmpeg setup.
